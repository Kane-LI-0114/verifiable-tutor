## Synthesis

Two evidence sets. The marketing set has five captures: three English homepages and two
Chinese corporate homepages. The product set has four captures: two Khan Academy surfaces,
one Brilliant course page, and one Coursera course page. The Chinese marketing captures are
weaker evidence for product UI; they are used for register and proof style. The Coursera
page, served in Chinese, is the better source for Chinese product labels.

Marketing norm:

- The reader is "you"; the product is a named role ("teaching assistant", "personal tutor").
- Headlines name a role, subject, or task in six to twelve words.
- Mechanism is a sequence of concrete actions: ask, sketch, guide, work through, check.
- Differentiation names the category failure: "without giving you direct answers", "not a
  cheating machine".
- Proof is specific when present: a number with a unit, a named survey with a method.

- Proof is specific when present: a number with a unit, a named survey with a method.

Product norm:

- Labels carry structure: "UNIT 16", "29 skills", "4 modules", "LEVEL 1". A label is usually
  a noun and a count, not a sentence.
- Progress is a legend of states, not a paragraph: "Mastered / Proficient / Familiar /
  Attempted / Not started".
- The action is an imperative: "Start Course challenge", "Do 4 problems", "Check", "Skip".
- Headings name what the reader gets: "What you'll learn", "Skills you'll gain",
  "Details to know", "Related content".
- Hierarchy is a breadcrumb, not prose.
- A next step is one sentence and a link: "Not feeling ready for this? Check out Get ready
  for Algebra 1."
- A subtitle earns its place only with a fact the controls do not show: "4 modules",
  "5 hours to complete".

Chinese marketing register observed:

- Scope is stated as a list of school stages and subjects ("小初高全科", "全学科").
- Audience is named in the block ("专为家长", "专为小学家长老师").
- Mission sentences are long, four-character heavy, and carry no mechanism ("以科技推动大规模
  因材施教"). Useful as a negative example, not a model.
- Proof is a named benchmark or ranking. This is the one pattern worth keeping.

Chinese product register observed on Coursera:

- Short labels, noun first: "您将学到什么", "您将获得的技能", "单元详情".
- Numbers stay numerals with a unit: "4个模块", "第 1 单元", "5 小时 完成".
- The page label names the object or the action. It does not start with "关于".
